Master Degree in Marine Biology (LM6)

UNIVPM, Ancona, Italy · 110/110 cum laude
Thesis: "Meiofauna biodiversity in seagrass meadows of the Central Adriatic Sea (Gabicce Mare)"
Supervisor: Prof. Roberto Danovaro; Co-supervisor: Prof.ssa Cristina Gambi

The Master's Degree in Marine Biology at the Polytechnic University of Marche is an international programme taught entirely in English and integrated with the International Master of Science in Marine Biological Resources (IMBRSea) network, attracting students and researchers from around the world. The programme combines advanced theoretical training with extensive laboratory, field, and research experience, providing a multidisciplinary understanding of marine ecosystems, biodiversity, conservation, and sustainable ocean management.

Throughout the programme, I developed expertise in marine ecology, biological and physical oceanography, marine conservation, fisheries biology, marine genomics, ecotoxicology, marine spatial planning, ecosystem restoration, and the design and management of Marine Protected Areas. Practical training included ecological field sampling, biodiversity monitoring, marine community surveys, scientific diving, GIS applications, and quantitative ecological data analysis.

My Master's research was conducted within the European DIVERSEA project under the supervision of Prof. Roberto Danovaro and Prof. Cristina Gambi, focusing on biodiversity patterns of meiofaunal communities associated with Mediterranean seagrass ecosystems. This research provided extensive experience in ecological fieldwork, laboratory analyses, taxonomic identification, statistical analysis, and scientific reporting, strengthening my interest in combining marine research with biodiversity conservation.
