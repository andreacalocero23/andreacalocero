EUREMAP Summer School on Marine Bioprospecting

Ghent University, Belgium · Marine@UGent

Selected as an international participant in the EUREMAP Summer School on Marine Bioprospecting, an intensive training programme organised by Marine@UGent (Ghent University) aimed at providing an interdisciplinary overview of the marine bioprospecting pipeline, from marine biodiversity exploration to the development of innovative and sustainable biotechnological applications.

The programme combined lectures, laboratory training, technical visits and discussions with leading researchers and industry professionals, covering topics including marine biotechnology, marine natural products, the Nagoya Protocol, the BBNJ Agreement, research valorisation, intellectual property, and the translation of scientific discoveries into industrial applications.

Practical laboratory sessions provided hands-on experience with analytical techniques commonly used in marine bioprospecting, including:

  • Assessment of temperature effects on the phenolic content of macroalgae.
  • Extraction and quantification of R-phycoerythrin from red seaweeds.
  • Detection of microbial siderophore production using the Chrome Azurol S (CAS) assay.

The programme also included technical visits to Marine@UGent research laboratories, the Bio Base Europe Pilot Plant, and Tech Lane Ghent Science Park, offering direct insight into how academic research is transferred to industrial-scale innovation and commercialization.

Beyond the technical training, the Summer School strengthened my understanding of the potential role of marine bioprospecting in supporting marine ecology, biodiversity conservation and ecosystem restoration through the sustainable use of marine biological resources. It also provided an excellent opportunity to collaborate and exchange ideas with MSc students, PhD candidates, researchers and professionals from diverse scientific backgrounds and countries, fostering international networking and interdisciplinary collaboration.
