ISPRA Summer School "Marine-Coastal Ecosystems and Climate Change: Connections and Resilience"

ISPRA, hosted at OGS Miramare; Trieste, Italy (14-18 September 2026)

Participated in ISPRA's five-day Summer School "Marine-Coastal Ecosystems and Climate Change: Connections and Resilience", held in Trieste from 14 to 18 September 2026 and hosted at OGS Miramare. The programme combined lectures, practical workshops, group work and field visits, connecting marine and coastal science with applied environmental management.

The sessions explored climate-change impacts on marine and coastal ecosystems, coastal hazards and defence, coastal geology, ecological status in lagoons and tidal environments, marine biodiversity, fisheries, habitats, and the restoration of marine forests.

Practical sessions introduced workflows for finding, downloading, preprocessing and visualising Copernicus environmental data, as well as how to select datasets for a specific case study.

Field visits included the Miramare Marine Protected Area, the Grado-Marano Lagoon and the Banco della Mula di Muggia, offering direct insight into protected marine areas, lagoon ecosystems and coastal geomorphology.

As part of a multidisciplinary team, I worked on a case study of coastal erosion in San Rossore, Tuscany. We assessed environmental and climate-related drivers, developed a hypothetical coastal management intervention and presented our results for discussion.

The Summer School strengthened my understanding of climate-related coastal risks, ecosystem resilience, environmental data and science-based coastal management. It also provided the opportunity to collaborate with participants, researchers and professionals from different scientific backgrounds.
