Functional diversity, trophic structure and community composition of nematode assemblages associated with Adriatic seagrass meadows

Postgraduate Research Intern · UNIVPM, Ancona, Italy
Marine Ecology Meiofauna Nematodes Benthic Ecology Ecological Indicators Multivariate Analysis

Project Overview

This post-graduate internship expanded my MSc research by investigating free-living marine nematode assemblages as indicators of benthic ecosystem functioning in Adriatic seagrass meadows.

Study area: Gabicce Mare (Central Adriatic Sea)
Habitats: Vegetated sediments dominated by Zostera marina, Zostera noltei, Cymodocea nodosa and adjacent unvegetated sediments.

Research objectives: Assess how habitat characteristics and environmental gradients influence nematode biodiversity, trophic structure, functional diversity and species composition.

Research Questions

🌿

How do seagrass meadows influence nematode assemblages?

Investigating the role of habitat structure and vegetation complexity in shaping benthic meiofaunal communities.

📊

Which environmental variables drive community patterns?

Identifying key environmental factors (sediment characteristics, organic matter, vegetation cover) influencing community composition.

🍽️

Do trophic groups respond differently between habitats?

Exploring functional diversity and trophic structure shifts across vegetated and unvegetated sediments.

🔬

Can nematodes act as ecological indicators?

Evaluating the potential of nematode assemblages as bioindicators of benthic ecosystem health and environmental change.

Methods

Taxonomic Identification

Free-living marine nematodes identified to genus/species level using morphological characters and taxonomic keys.

Ecological Indices

Species richness, ES(51), Pielou evenness, Maturity Index (MI), Index of Trophic Diversity (ITD).

Functional Analysis

Wieser trophic groups (1A, 1B, 2A, 2B) classification for functional diversity assessment.

Statistics

PERMANOVA, NMDS, SIMPER, DISTLM for multivariate community analysis.

Software

PRIMER, R, Excel for data processing, statistical analysis and visualization.
