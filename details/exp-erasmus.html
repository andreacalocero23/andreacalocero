Youth Lead Local Development (YLLD)

Erasmus+ International Mobility Programme
Location: Potenza, Italy Period: September 2023 Organisation: ASCAE • Erasmus+

About the Programme

This was an Erasmus+ international mobility programme bringing together participants from different European countries to develop competences in community-led local development, active citizenship and youth participation.

The mobility followed a preparatory training course held in July 2023 and concluded with the award of the official Youthpass Certificate. The project aimed to improve the participation of young people living in rural and remote areas through innovative community-based approaches.

About the Project

The Youth Lead Local Development (YLLD) project aimed to promote community-led local development, youth participation, sustainable rural development, and social innovation through non-formal education and international cooperation.

Key themes included leadership, active citizenship, and the development of participatory methodologies to empower young people in rural and remote areas. The project promoted lifelong learning while strengthening young people's capacity to become active contributors within their own local communities.

Learning Outcomes

This experience strengthened my intercultural communication, leadership, team collaboration and critical thinking. Working alongside participants from different European backgrounds enhanced my capacity to work in multicultural environments and deepened my understanding of participatory development processes.

I became more aware of the role young people can play in shaping sustainable local communities, and I learned how non-formal education can be a powerful tool for social change. The project also reinforced my belief in the importance of international cooperation and active citizenship in addressing shared challenges.
